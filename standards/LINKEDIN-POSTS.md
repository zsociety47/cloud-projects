# LinkedIn post strategy for cloud proof-of-concept projects

Standing instructions for drafting and publishing four posts per project. Frame every project as your own proof of concept. Never mention a mentorship or program. Never use "Lab 01" style naming, and never "lab" in public-facing text. Use the project's real name.

Audience: mid-level cloud and DevOps practitioners and hiring managers. Tone: storytelling, specific, no hype.

## Post style

- The visual does the work. Body is about 300–500 characters.
- Punchy one-line sentences. No long paragraphs, no long bullet lists.
- End with a question so people comment.
- Exactly five hashtags: three specific, two broad.
- Links go in the post body, never only in the comments.

## Four posts

1. **Video.** Upload the raw video file to LinkedIn (never a Loom link). Thumbnail: still frame from the video with short overlay text, 1920×1080 PNG.
2. **GitHub.** Screenshot of the repo (file structure or README). Repo link in the body.
3. **Architecture.** The diagram, following [DIAGRAM-STANDARD.md](DIAGRAM-STANDARD.md).
4. **Tool.** One tool from the project. Visual is a logo, screenshot, or terminal output.

Optional fifth post only if the project has another strong angle.

Save drafts to `docs/linkedin/` in the project repository: `01-video.md`, `02-github.md`, `03-architecture.md`, `04-tool.md`.

## Video recording guide

Check every recording script and every finished video against this before it becomes post 1.

- **Face on camera** in the intro, the build or walkthrough, and the outro. Screen share with a voiceover is not enough.
- **Intro** (same every time), all in the first 30 seconds: name and who you are; what you are building; the business problem it solves and why that matters.
- **Middle**
  - Flow A, under 15 minutes: build it live on camera, explaining how and why.
  - Flow B, over 15 minutes: build it off camera first, then walk through the finished architecture.
- **Outro** (same every time): the business use case and why it matters; what you would change or improve if you did it again.

The intro sets the business context, the middle proves you can execute, the outro shows you think critically.

## Publishing

- 8:00 AM Eastern, one post per weekday
- After publishing: engage with three recent posts under a specific hashtag; leave a useful comment on at least two
